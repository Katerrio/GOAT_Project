Who is V?

We don't know. Fixer's would rather die than give you any information on their clients. Some some sources say that they're name is Vincent. Other sources say that they're name is Valerie. The only concrete evidence that we have is they they go by the moniker "V".

We only know that "V" one of the most mysterious mercs to take the world by storm. They are only known through their fixers, and how their jobs are done with either the utmost precision or complete chaos. The only known pattern between them is that it seems as if they are having a scary amount of joy in what they do.

If you spot or become targeted by this merc, keep these thoughts in mind:

  • Bodies found as if they had killed themselves.
  • Bodies scarred with electrical burns.
  • Bodies with destroyed synapses.

Intense Knowledge of Night City:

V knows all of Night City and the Badlands like the back of their hand. They have avoided police capture and capture of rival gangs/mercs through maneuvering through Night Citie's backroads, roof tops, main roads, and alleys. They treat the city like thier own personal jungle gym which has led to more mystery surround their appearance.

Additionally, it seems as if they are connected to Night City's police database. They have appeared at crime scenes before we had the chance to arrive. They tend to have some knowledge on their targets' Cyberware, bounties on their head, past crimes, and weak spots. How this individual(or group) got access to our database is unknown. We are currently working on patching this.

First recorded instance of V:

"V" was likely a small time pusher that rose the ranks in secret. The first time their name reached NCPD status was when they killed Saburo Arasaka - the King of Night City. They broke into Konpeki Plaza with a small crew of amateur runners:

  • Jackie Welles - (Solo)
  • T-Bug - (Netrunner)
Gotten together by washed up Fixer:
  • Dexter Deshawn

This crew of misfits stole The Relic from Arasaka, killed Saburo, and tried to escape. Luckily, the crew's plans were dismantled by Yorinobu, and they were hunted and killed by Arasaka's top bodyguards, Saburo's personal bodyguard Goro Takemura, and Night City's very own living nuke, Adam Smasher.

The entire crew and fixer were killed in the pursuit...
